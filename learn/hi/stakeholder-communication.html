🗣️ Non-technical logon ko AI samjhaana

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Asli zindagi ki misaal

Achha CA dukaandaar ko tax law section by section nahi samjhaata. Woh kehta hai: 'Agar aap X karoge, to is saal ₹40,000 bachenge, aur ek risk yeh hai.' Business logon ko AI samjhaana bhi aisa hi hai: nateeje se shuru karo, unke shabd use karo, aur risks ke baare mein imaandaar raho.

💡 Aasaan shabdon mein

Forward deployed engineers, PMs aur consultants ka bahut time aise logon ke saath jaata hai jinhe embeddings se matlab nahi. Unhe time, paisa, risk aur apne customers se matlab hai. Updates aise structure karo: nateeja ya chahiye decision, saboot (demo ya numbers), risks aur unhe kaise sambhaal rahe ho, phir next steps. Jargon ki jagah analogies use karo. Working prototype jaldi dikhao, kyunki woh das slides se behtar hai. Discovery meetings mein bolne se zyaada suno, aur requirements likhit mein confirm karo. AI kya nahi kar sakta, yeh pehle hi saaf batao: bharosa aapki sabse keemti cheez hai.

🪜 Step by step

  1. Us nateeje se shuru karo jiski unhe parwah hai: bacha time, paisa, risk, customers.
  2. Kaise kaam karta hai ek rozmarra ki misaal se samjhao, technical shabdon se nahi.
  3. Limits ke baare mein imaandaar raho: kitni baar galat hota hai, aur usse kya bachata hai.
  4. Batao mat, dikhao: unke asli data pe chhota demo slides se zyaada convince karta hai.
  5. Ek saaf decision ya agle step ke saath khatam karo jo aapko unse chahiye.

🔑 Zaroori shabd

Stakeholder
Koi bhi jo project se affect hota ho ya uspe decide karta ho.
Discovery
Asli problem, users aur constraints samajhne ki meetings.
Executive summary
3–4 line ka version: nateeja, saboot, risk, maang.

👀 Example dekho

text
❌ "We implemented hybrid retrieval with a re-ranker and improved MRR@10 by 0.18."
✅ "The assistant now finds the right policy 9 times out of 10, up from 7.
    Agents save ~4 minutes per ticket (≈ ₹6 lakh/month).
    Risk: rare wrong answers → it always shows its source.
    Decision needed: roll out to 2 more teams?"

Kaam same hai, bas nateeja → saboot → risk → maang ki tarah samjhaaya gaya.

⚠️ Aam galtiyaan jinse bachna hai

  • Zyaada vaada karna ("yeh 100% accurate hoga"). Pehli galti pe hi bharosa toot jaata hai.
  • Logon ko aise metrics mein dubona jo woh samajh na sakein. Numbers ko impact mein translate karo.

✅ Jaldi se check karo

Business leader ko update dete waqt shuruaat kis se karni chahiye?

  1. Aapka tech stack
  2. Model architecture ki details
  3. Business nateeja aur aapko kaunsa decision chahiye
Jawaab dekho

C. Business nateeja aur aapko kaunsa decision chahiye: Leaders nateejon aur risks pe decide karte hain. Pehle woh do, details maangne pe.

🙋 Seekhne wale yeh poochhte hain

Business leader ko accuracy kaise samjhaun?

Ginti aur nateejon mein: "100 invoices mein se 95 sahi padhta hai; baaki 5 ek insaan ke check ke liye flag hote hain."

Agar stakeholder ko dar ho ki AI unki team ko replace kar dega?

Maano, dikhao tool kaise boring kaam hataata hai, team ko testing mein shaamil karo, aur batao roles kaise badlenge.

🎯 Is topic pe interviews mein poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pehle khud bolkar jawaab dene ki koshish karo, phir model answer kholo.

Customer kehta hai, "Humein AI use karna hai." Pehli meeting mein kya karoge?

💬

Main AI se nahi, unki problem se shuru karta hoon. Poochhta hoon: abhi sabse dard bhara ya mehenga process kaunsa hai? Kaun karta hai, kitni baar, aur success kaise naapte hain? Kaunsa data aur systems involved hain? Phir ek do use cases chunta hoon jo high value hon, jinka data available ho aur AI thoda galat ho to kam risk ho, aur ek success metric pe sehmati banata hoon, jaise "invoice processing time 50% kam". Meeting ek saaf agle step pe khatam: kuch hafton mein asli data ke saath chhota proof of concept.

✅

  • Pehle problem: dard, kitni baar, owner, current metric
  • Available data aur systems
  • Use cases value, feasibility aur risk se chuno
  • Naapne layak goal aur chhota agla step tay karo

: Customer discovery. Dekhte hain solve karne se pehle poochhte ho ya nahi.

Customer aisa feature maangta hai jo aapko lagta hai kaam nahi karega. Kaise sambhaaloge?

💬

Pehle samjho woh kyun chahte hain; aksar request ke peeche asli zaroorat hoti hai jo doosre tarike se poori ho sakti hai. Phir apni chinta saboot ke saath batao, raay ke saath nahi: jaise "aapke 100 documents pe test mein fully automatic approval 12% baar galat tha". Aisa alternative do jo asli goal poora kare, jaise automatic drafts aur ek click approval. Phir bhi try karna chahein to agreed success criteria ke saath chhota, time boxed pilot propose karo, taaki data decide kare. Izzat se aur unke result pe focus rakho.

✅

  • Asli zaroorat dhoondho
  • Data use karo, behtar ho to unka data
  • Goal poora karne wala alternative do
  • Agreed criteria ke saath time boxed experiment

: Bina authority ke influence; customer ke saath maturity.

Customer ke saamne aapka AI demo fail ho gaya. Kya karoge?

💬

Shaant raho aur imaandaar: "Yeh galat jawaab hai, chaliye dikhata hoon kyun hua." Phir use use karo: trace kholo, dikhao kaunsa document retrieve hua aur kahan galat hua, aur samjhao evaluation set se ise kaise pakdenge aur theek karenge. Customers un logon pe zyaada bharosa karte hain jo apne system ki galtiyaan samajhte hain, bajaye unke jo dikhaate hain ki koi galti nahi hoti. Baad mein case eval set mein jodo, theek karo, aur result ke saath follow up karo. Agli baar unke asli data se rehearse karo aur ek recorded backup rakho.

✅

  • Shaant raho, maano
  • Ise control dikhaane mein badlo: trace, samjhao, fix ka plan
  • Fix ke saath follow up
  • Bachaav: asli data se rehearse, backup recording

: Pressure mein sanyam aur imaandaari.

Ek logistics company missed deliveries kam karna chahti hai. Kaise approach karoge?

💬

Solve karne se pehle clarify: missed delivery kise kehte hain, kitni hoti hain, kitne ki padti hain, aur kaunsa data hai, jaise GPS, delivery attempts, addresses aur customer contacts? Problem ko wajahon mein todo: galat addresses, customer ghar pe nahi, routing aur time windows, aur capacity. Har ek ka asar unke data se naapo, phir sabse tez jeet propose karo. Jaise order ke time addresses saaf aur verify karne ke liye LLM plus geocoding, aur no show predict karne wala model jo ek din pehle confirmation message bheje. Metric tay karo, ek shehar mein control group ke saath pilot, phir scale. Poore waqt dekho ki operations staff asal mein kya use karega.

✅

  • Definitions, scale, cost aur data clarify
  • Root causes mein todo; data se har ek naapo
  • Tez, high impact interventions chuno (AI aur non AI)
  • Control group ke saath pilot; metric; scale up

: FDE roles ka main filter round decomposition case hai.

Kisi teammate ya stakeholder ke saath asehmati ke baare mein batao.

💬

Dikhao ki aap data aur izzat ke saath asehmat hote ho. "Ek product manager humara AI reply feature saare customers ke liye launch karna chahte the. Mujhe chinta thi kyunki humare tests mein woh kabhi kabhi refund policies gadh deta tha. Seedha na kehne ke bajaye maine paanch asli examples aur eval set ka error rate dikhaya, aur agent approval ke saath 10% tickets pe launch propose kiya. Humne badhaane ke criteria pe sehmati banayi. Do hafte baad error rate threshold se neeche tha aur humne badhaaya. Rishta accha raha kyunki humne common goal pe focus kiya."

✅

  • Situation aur dono nazariye imaandaari se
  • Authority nahi, saboot use kiya
  • Samjhauta ya experiment
  • Result aur baad ka rishta

: Collaboration aur influence.

Koi technical concept aise samjhao jaise main technical nahi hoon.

💬

Apne kaam se kuch chuno aur rozmarra ki misaal do. Jaise RAG samjhaana: "Socho ek bahut smart naya employee jisne bahut padha hai par humari company ke baare mein kuch nahi jaanta. Policy ka sawaal poochho to woh confidently guess kar sakta hai. RAG aisa hai jaise jawaab se pehle use humari policy manual ke sahi pages de dena, aur page dikhane ko kehna. To jawaab humare asli documents pe based hote hain, aur aap check kar sakte ho." Phir samajh check karo: "Kya yeh samajh aaya?"

✅

  • Rozmarra ki misaal
  • Jargon nahi, ya samjhaaya hua
  • Sunne wale ke liye kyun zaroori
  • Samajh check karo

: Communication, FDE, PM aur senior roles ke liye zaroori.

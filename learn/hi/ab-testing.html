🆎 AI features ke liye metrics aur A/B testing

EnglishHinglish

🏠 Asli zindagi ki misaal

Chai stall wala sochta hai ki nayi adrak wali recipe zyaada bikegi ya nahi. Poori tarah badal kar pichhle mahine se compare karna galat hoga, kyunki mausam aur tyohaar sales badal dete hain. Iski jagah do hafte tak woh aadhe customers ko random tarah se purani recipe deta hai aur aadhon ko nayi, phir compare karta hai. Yahi A/B test hai.

💡 Aasaan shabdon mein

AI feature launch karne se pehle uska success metric define karo (resolution rate, time bachaya, conversion), guardrail metrics jo kharab nahi hone chahiye (complaints, cost, latency), aur kitna improvement matter karta hai. A/B test users ko random tarah se control (purana) aur treatment (naya) groups mein baantta hai, taaki dono mein systematic fark sirf feature ka ho, aur aap causation claim kar sako. Itni der chalao ki statistical significance mile, aur beech mein jhaank kar jaldi band mat karo. AI ke liye launch se pehle offline evals aur launch ke baad online A/B results, dono milaao.

🎬 Dekho kaise kaam karta hai

Users random tarah se baante jaate hain: aadhe purana version (A) dekhte hain, aadhe naya (B). Split random hai, isliye results ka fark feature ki wajah se hai.

🪜 Step by step

  1. Hypothesis likho: "AI reply drafts handling time 15% kam karenge, satisfaction giraaye bina."
  2. Ek primary metric aur kuch guardrail metrics chuno jo bigadne nahi chahiye.
  3. Users (ya agents) ko randomly control aur treatment mein baanto, aur andaaza lagao kitne chahiye aur kitne din.
  4. Poori planned duration chalao, beech mein jhaank kar jaldi band mat karo.
  5. Confidence intervals ke saath fark analyse karo, guardrails aur segments check karo, phir decide karo.

🔑 Zaroori shabd

Control / treatment
Bina change wala group vs change wala group.
Guardrail metric
Metric jo kharab nahi hona chahiye jab aap doosra optimise karo.
Statistical significance
Bharosa ki fark sirf random kismat nahi hai.

👀 Example dekho

text
Feature: AI-suggested replies for support agents
Success:   handling time  9.1 → 6.8 min (treatment) ✅ significant
Guardrail: CSAT           4.4 → 4.4                 ✅ unchanged
Guardrail: cost/ticket    +₹1.2                     ✅ within budget
Decision: roll out to 100%

Ek success metric, guardrails, aur saaf go / no-go faisla.

⚠️ Aam galtiyaan jinse bachna hai

  • Results acche dikhte hi band kar dena. Jaldi jhaankne se jhoothi jeet milti hai.
  • Bahut saare metrics test karna aur jo hila uska jashn manana. Primary metric pehle tay karo.

✅ Jaldi se check karo

A/B test mein users ko random tarah se kyun baantte hain?

  1. Taaki groups ke beech sirf feature ka systematic fark ho
  2. Test chhota karne ke liye
  3. Code karna aasaan hai
Jawaab dekho

A. Taaki groups ke beech sirf feature ka systematic fark ho: Randomisation baaki sab balance kar deta hai, isliye fark feature ki wajah se maana ja sakta hai.

🙋 Seekhne wale yeh poochhte hain

Statistical significance simple shabdon mein kya hai?

Agar change ka koi asli asar na hota to yeh fark kitna unlikely hota. Yeh nahi batata ki asar bada ya keemti hai.

Agar randomise na kar sakein to?

Region ya team ke hisaab se phased rollout karo, pehle aur baad dhyaan se compare karo, aur imaandaari se maano ki saboot kamzor hai.

🎯 Is topic pe interviews mein poochhe jaane wale sawaal

Pehle khud bolkar jawaab dene ki koshish karo, phir model answer kholo.

Correlation aur causation mein kya fark hai? Causation kaise prove karoge?

💬

Correlation matlab do cheezein saath badhti ghatti hain; causation matlab ek doosre ko cause karti hai. Ice cream ki bikri aur doobne ke cases saath badhte hain kyunki dono garmi mein badhte hain, ice cream doobne ki wajah nahi. Products mein, naya feature use karne wale users zyaada tik sakte hain sirf isliye kyunki woh pehle se zyaada engaged the. Causation dikhane ka best tool randomised A/B test hai: feature randomly aadhe users ko do, taaki systematic fark sirf feature ka ho, phir results compare karo.

✅

  • Correlation ≠ causation; chhupe teesre factors (confounders)
  • Selection bias: opt in karne wale alag hote hain
  • Randomised experiment sabse pakka tarika

: Decisions ke liye statistical soch; PMs aur analysts ke liye zaroori.

Feed ranking ya search jaise end-to-end ML system design interview ko kaise structure karte ho?

💬

Main chhe steps ka structure use karta hoon. Pehle, business objective, latency budget (maan lo 150 ms) aur scale clarify karo. Doosra, ML task frame karo aur offline metrics (jaise Recall@100 aur NDCG@10) plus online north-star aur guardrail metrics chuno. Teesra, data aur feature store batao: user history, item metadata, aur real-time session signals jo bina leakage ke point-in-time jude hon. Chautha, do-stage funnel use karo: tez candidate generation (two-tower embeddings + BM25 jo millions items ko 500 pe laaye) aur uske baad rich GBDT ya neural ranker jo un 500 ko score kare, plus diversity aur business rules ke liye re-ranker. Paanchwa, cold start aur position bias cover karo. Chhatha, shadow/A-B rollout aur drift monitoring.

✅

  • Pehle business goal, scale aur latency budget clarify karo
  • Offline ranking metrics (Recall@K, NDCG) vs online business aur guardrail metrics
  • Two-stage architecture: tez candidate retrieval → feature-rich ranker → business rules
  • Cold start, position bias, point-in-time features aur monitoring cover karo

: Dekhta hai ki aap sirf loss function ke bajaye poora production ML funnel design kar sakte ho ya nahi.

Naya model offline behtar dikha par production ki chinta hai. Safely rollout kaise karoge?

💬

Step by step. Pehle shadow mode: naya model asli traffic pe predictions karta hai, par users ko purane model ka output milta hai, to predictions, latency aur errors safely compare kar sakta hoon. Phir canary: chhota hissa, jaise 5%, traffic naye model ko bhejo aur technical aur business metrics dekho. Theek rahe to dheere badhao, ya proper A/B test chalao. Purana model ready rakho taaki rollback ek switch ho. Success aur rollback criteria shuru karne se pehle tay karo.

✅

  • Shadow deployment
  • Chhote traffic pe canary, phir badhao ya A/B test
  • Pehle se tay success aur rollback criteria
  • Ek click rollback

: Production mein risk management.

AI feature ke success metrics kaise define karoge?

💬

Layers mein, taaki koi ek number problems na chhupaaye. Model quality: eval set pe accuracy, hallucination rate ya rubric scores. User outcome: kya madad mili? Task completion, bacha time, users AI output kitna edit karte hain. Adoption aur engagement: kitne eligible users try karte hain aur wapas aate hain. Business: revenue, per ticket cost ya retention. Saath mein guardrail metrics jo bigadne nahi chahiye, jaise complaints, latency aur per use cost. Bahut accurate feature jo koi use na kare woh bhi fail hai, aur popular feature jiska error rate zyaada ho woh risk hai.

✅

  • Layers: model quality, user outcome, adoption, business
  • Guardrails: errors, latency, cost, complaints
  • Leading vs lagging metrics
  • Koi metric akela nahi

: Sabse common AI PM analytical sawaal.

2 million products wali e commerce site ke liye recommendation system design karo.

💬

Clarify: recommendations kahan dikhengi, goal (clicks, purchases), latency budget aur available data. Do stages. Candidate generation: 2 million products ko jaldi kuch sau tak laao, collaborative filtering ya two tower embeddings plus category mein popular jaise simple sources se. Ranking: zyaada detailed model, jaise gradient boosting ya neural ranker, user, product aur context features se candidates score karta hai, phir business rules stock aur variety sambhaalte hain. Cold start: naye users ko popular aur contextual items; naye products ke liye category aur text embeddings jaise content features. Offline recall aur NDCG se evaluate, phir purchases pe online A/B test. Precomputed embeddings aur fresh features ke liye feature store se serve.

✅

  • Goal aur latency clarify
  • Do stage: candidate generation, phir ranking
  • Users aur items ka cold start
  • Offline metrics, phir A/B test; feature store ke saath serving

: Classic ML system design.

Humne AI tutor launch kiya. 10% se kam users ko pasand hai, zyaadatar ko bekaar lagta hai. Kya karoge?

💬

Pehle batwaara samjho. Woh 10% kaun hain: kaunsi class, subject ya use case? Aur baaki ke liye "bekaar" ka matlab kya: galat jawaab, bahut generic, bahut slow, ya jo chahiye tha woh nahi? Main asli conversations padhunga, dekhunga users kahan chhodte hain, aur dono groups se baat karunga. Agar 10% ka ek saaf use case hai, jaise maths ki exam revision, to wahan double down: us case ki quality evals se sudhaaro, aur onboarding badlo taaki naye users usi se shuru karein. Baaki ke liye top failure wajahein theek karo ya jin segments mein fit nahi wahan feature dikhaana band karo.

✅

  • Pasand karne wale vs baaki ko alag karo
  • Conversations, funnels, interviews se diagnose
  • Jo use case chal raha hai uspe double down
  • Top failures theek karo ya audience chhoti karo

: Ulajhe AI launch pe analytical product soch.

Aapka AI feature launch ho gaya. Kaise pata karoge ki woh sach mein kaam kar raha hai, aur use kaise sudhaaroge?

💬

Main layers mein naapta hoon. Woh outcome jiske liye feature bana, jaise handle time ya bina escalation ke solve hue tickets, A/B test ya holdout group se saabit. Usage: adoption, retention aur frequency. Behaviour pe based quality: accept rate, users drafts kitna edit karte hain, regenerate rate, chhodna aur escalation, saath mein wajah wala thumbs feedback, jo akele kamzor signal hai. Guardrails: shikaayatein, safety flags, latency aur cost kharab nahi hone chahiye. Har hafte privacy rules ke andar asli interactions ka sample lete hain, failures ko type se label karte hain, aur sabse bade types ko naye eval cases aur fixes mein badalte hain. Yahi loop launch ke baad feature ko sudhaarta rehta hai.

✅

  • A/B ya holdout se saabit outcome metric
  • Usage: adoption, retention, frequency
  • Edit aur accept rate jaise behaviour signals akele thumbs se behtar
  • Guardrails: shikaayatein, safety, latency, cost
  • Har hafte failure review jo eval set mein jaata hai

: Product sense aur launch ke baad ki ownership.
